01 The Bubble Standard

Two people,
a squeegee each,
and a 25-year habit
of doing it properly.

Bubble Truck has been cleaning glass in King and Pierce Counties since before water-fed poles were common here. We still hand-wash every pane, then finish with pure, filtered water so nothing dries in a streak — including the three-storey stuff most crews skip.

Windows are what we are known for, but the same crew clears gutters and downspouts before the rainy season and hangs (then removes) Christmas lights for a few hundred local homes each winter. Upfront pricing, on-time arrival, and a 24-hour window to tell us if something was missed.

07 Good questions

Before we
squeegee.

Still unsure about something? Call Adam directly — you will get a person, not a phone tree.

(206) 423-7408

Exterior glass every two to three months keeps Puget Sound rain film and pollen from bonding to the surface; interiors every three to six months is plenty for most homes. Homes near busy roads or sprinklers usually benefit from the more frequent schedule.

All of King and Pierce Counties — Renton, Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland, Kent, Auburn, Burien, Des Moines, Maple Valley, Tacoma and the towns between. If you are outside the usual route, call and we will tell you honestly whether we can get to you.

For exterior-only work you can leave us a gate code and go about your day. Interiors need someone on site — we would rather ask than assume. We are happy to work around naps, calls and dogs.

Yes — we hold a current Washington business licence and carry liability insurance. Ask and we will send the details; you should never have to take a contractor's word for it.

We use biodegradable, pet-safe solutions and keep gates the way we found them. Indoor pets are happiest in another room while we work, and we will happily give the dog a hello when we are done.

Window count, number of storeys and access, screens and tracks, skylights, and any hard-water or overspray removal. You get a flat quote before we book — the number does not move because a job took longer than expected.

Puget Sound weather is part of the job. Because exteriors are finished with pure water and not soap, a light shower will not undo the clean. If conditions make the work unsafe or pointless, we call you and rebook at no charge.
